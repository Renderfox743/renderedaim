This trainer is built for a real mouse and keyboard. Touch input cannot replicate the information density needed to build transferable aim.

• Touch has lower information density than a mouse
• No pointer lock means sensitivity settings don't apply
• Right-click scoping and Shift-ADS are impossible
• Aim assist cannot be trained without a mouse
